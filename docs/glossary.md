# Design glossary

- **Top panel**: black upper surface surrounding the switches and dial.
- **Lower shell / diffuser base**: frosted translucent enclosure part intended to spread light from LEDs beneath the PCB. The whole lower case is intended to be heavily frosted and house the electronics.
- **Underglow**: light cast beneath the case onto the desk, especially near the front/hand edge. Distinct from illuminated key legends.
- **Riser bar**: detachable support with a triangular side profile, accepted to span the rear underside and increase typing angle. Use one alternative 4° or 7° bar at a time; remove for flat use.
- **Magnetic seat**: nominal captive pockets and locating keys between case and riser; retention force, fit and assembly access remain unvalidated.
- **Setting feedback**: temporary lighting indication of volume, brightness or profile changes before returning to ambient lighting.
- **Appearance study**: editable Blender geometry and renders used to agree on form; not accepted manufacturing CAD or tested optics/electronics.
