# Low-profile switches and interchangeable legends

Status: proposed; owner clarification and component checks pending.
Date: 2026-10-18

## Request

Explore low-profile clicky switches and a removable sheet similar to a laptop keyboard protector, over the existing normally labelled keycaps, displaying macro symbols on the removable sheet. Blank replacement keycaps are not required. Reference supplied by owner: https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0GHR7ZMHH. The product page was inaccessible during research; no material, thickness, construction or fit has been verified.

## Clarified intent

- Keep the normal labelled keycaps for keyboard/gaming mode.
- Add one removable sheet with macro icons/shapes over the normal keycaps.
- The supplied silhouette has a stepped upper-right notch, provisionally interpreted as knob clearance.
- Detect the installed sheet and switch to macro-pad mode automatically. NFC or another detection method is acceptable in principle; no mechanism is selected.

## Open questions

- Confirm the notch clears the top-right knob.
- One macro overlay/profile versus multiple identified overlays/profiles.
- Opacity needed to hide original legends and whether macro symbols must remain illuminated.
- How the cover handles the dial and 2u Space, preserves backlighting and changes key feel.
- Suitable low-profile clicky switch family, PCB footprint, low-profile keycap stem and optical compatibility.

## Scope

The v4 USB-flush variant is the last accepted visual baseline. Do not treat its standard-height switch geometry, keycaps or earlier MX assumptions as compatible with the new low-profile direction. Physical legend swapping does not change key actions; software profiles do that separately. No revised Blender geometry or manufacturing decisions are approved yet.
