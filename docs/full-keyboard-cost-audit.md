## Critical decisions before a firm total

1. **Close electronics and power before PCB release.** The selected driver has 36 independently controlled constant-current PWM channels; its external current-setting resistor and support circuit belong in the BOM. Its Rev H revision history records an **NRND watermark** added in 2021: obtain lifecycle/stock confirmation and review suitability before release, without silently changing the part. The manufacturer's reel quantity is not a one-off distributor MOQ. [Lumissil datasheet, application circuit, ordering information and revision history](https://www.lumissil.com/assets/pdf/core/IS31FL3236_DS.pdf).
2. **Set a usable USB lighting limit.** Adafruit specifies up to 60 mA per pixel for this strip. Fourteen pixels therefore give a conservative **840 mA strip-only maximum**, before white keys/controller. This arithmetic is a power-design warning, not measured use; define brightness/current limiting and verify the actual port/cable budget. The strip guide also identifies data resistance, buffering and level shifting; final component values/volume must suit this small enclosure. [Strip specification](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2969), [wiring practices](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-neopixel-uberguide/best-practices), [selected TI buffer](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74AHCT1G125).
3. **Quote a manufacture-ready revision.** Existing caps, USB skin, nut-trap walls, clamps/capture rings and material interfaces need review/redesign; frozen geometry is a cost study. Validate samples for full switch travel, stem fit, 2u movement, retention and diffusion. Robu publishes process guidance, but a calculator price does not accept these interfaces. [Local manufacturing audit](../procurement/robu/README.md), [Robu printing guide](https://robu.in/3d-printing-guide/).
4. **Resolve PCB thickness and files.** Recorded Robu calculator refused a price without Gerbers and offered 1.6 mm rather than the model's 1.5 mm. Ask for the selected stack or revalidate the stabilizer/mechanical stack before accepting an alternative. Quote the actual batch separately from one-board consumption. [Recorded calculator result](robu-quote-status.md), [Robu PCB service](https://robu.in/product/online-pcb-manufacturing-service/).
5. **Count software as delivery work.** QMK Raw HID supports bidirectional host communication and requires a host program; Windows endpoint-volume callbacks can supply real volume changes. This supports the accepted helper approach but is not implemented keyboard firmware or a tested Windows app. [QMK Raw HID](https://docs.qmk.fm/features/rawhid), [Microsoft endpoint volume notifications](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/coreaudio/endpoint-volume-controls).

## Purchase-budget rules and quote completion

Keep three separate figures: **one-keyboard materials consumed**, **prototype cash spend** (packs/MOQs/spares, complete strip reel, PCB batch, freight and taxes), and **development/validation cost** (engineering, samples, tools and rework). Do not amortize a five-board purchase into one fifth of the money needed to build the first unit. Existing owned tools/materials need an explicit declaration before treating their additional cash spend as zero. No speculative percentage contingency is presented as a supplier quote.

For every unpriced line obtain: exact part/material/revision, installed quantity, purchasable quantity/pack/MOQ, unit and extended INR prices, GST included/excluded, availability, delivery destination/freight, import/clearance if applicable, lead time, assembly/finishing inclusion, quote expiry, and accepted tolerances/DFM exceptions. Add GST once; never apply it again to the Robu/Meckeys inclusive figures. Deduplicate kit contents and supplier/assembly turnkey component charges.

**Optional, separate from the base working-keyboard bill:** profile sheets (forming/tooling, opaque masking and printed icons, knob notch, key-travel trials), per-sheet identification tags and reader/antenna/support circuitry, plus profile-detection firmware. Automatic NFC switching and formed overlays remain concepts. The alternate 7° bar and per-key RGB comparison also remain separate options, not extra mandatory cost.
